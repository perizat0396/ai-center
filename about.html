About us

We turn students into AI founders.

AI Center is the startup lab of Sarsen Amanzholov East Kazakhstan University — a place where ideas get mentors, compute and a team.

Our story ↓

Our mission

We believe the next great AI products can start in a university classroom. Our job is to remove everything that stands between a student and a working product — the lack of hardware, of mentors, of a team, of courage.

Our story

Opened in 2025,
growing every semester

  1. 2025

    The lab opens

    Sarsen Amanzholov University opens AI Center — a space, GPUs and mentors for students who want to build with AI.

  2. 2025

    First teams

    The first student teams form around real problems in medicine, agriculture and education.

  3. 2026

    First Demo Day

    Teams pitch their prototypes to industry partners and investors for the first time.

  4. Today

    Growing fast

    New teams join every semester — and the lab keeps growing together with them.

What we offer

Everything you need
to build, in one place

  • Mentors

    Weekly sessions with engineers, researchers and founders who have shipped real products.

  • GPU compute

    Thousands of GPU hours a year to train and test your models — free for student teams.

  • Workspace

    An open lab on campus to work, meet your team and stay late when the model finally converges.

  • Network

    Partners, investors and alumni who help you go from Demo Day to your first customers.
